## - About Me

🎓 **Cybersecurity Student (India)**  
🔐 **Aspiring Penetration Tester | Ethical Hacker**  
🏴‍☠️ **CTF Player | Security Research Enthusiast**

I focus on **ethical hacking, penetration testing, and Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges**, with a strong interest in understanding *why vulnerabilities exist*, not just how to exploit them.

I actively practice in **lab-based and CTF environments**, working across **web, network, and system security**. My long-term goal is to become a professional **penetration tester and security researcher**, contributing to safer software and infrastructure.
